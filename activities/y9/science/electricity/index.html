About these models and your lesson sources

Lessons follow the teacher-supplied Static Electricity and charge Presentation (26 slides) and CircuitsPresentation (54 slides). The original pizza and obstacle illustrations are retained unchanged from those presentations. Resistance Run also uses edited background and character layers to animate that picture. Explanations and interactive artwork are written for this platform. Every needed instruction is on the page.

Models slow and enlarge charge motion. Real electrons have fast random motion and a small net drift; an electrical signal propagates much faster than individual electrons drift. Energy transfer involves fields. The pizza and playground models are analogies, not microscopic mechanisms. Ideal circuit meters, a regulated supply and controlled resistor temperatures omit experimental uncertainty.
