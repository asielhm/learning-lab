# Breathing Lab — Y9 Science

A standalone interactive lab with three sections: respiratory anatomy, adjustable ventilation, and guided alveolar gas exchange. The schematic 3D model is constructed procedurally with the same pinned Three.js version used by Heart Lab. No model download or build step is required. It is an original teaching reconstruction, not a scan or a physically calibrated physiological model.

The front-view right lung is on the viewer's left. The model shows a right lung with three lobes, a left lung with two and an indicative cardiac notch, trachea, main bronchi, bronchioles, rib outlines and a diaphragm. Shape, colour and scale are illustrative. The diaphragm contracts/flattens during inhalation and relaxes/domes during quiet exhalation; ribs move up/out during inhalation. Lungs expand passively rather than acting as skeletal muscle. The controls show pressure relative to the outside and relative model expansion, not numerical clinical pressures or lung capacities. Rate is adjustable from 6–30 breaths/min and illustrative depth from 20–100%.

Gas exchange is an original SVG section: oxygen passes from alveolar air to blood, carbon dioxide from blood to alveolar air. Four guided investigations require tracing the appropriate gas and answering a check. Breathing, gas exchange and aerobic cellular respiration are explicitly distinguished. The section is schematic and particle paths are illustrative.

The separate `revision.html` page links to the topic's multiple-choice study simulator. Platform breadcrumbs return to the catalogue.

## Checks

- Identify all six structures using the buttons or the 3D model; verify correct/incorrect question feedback.
- Enter Make a breath. Verify Play/Pause, rate/depth changes, hold at inhale/exhale, pressure at the turnarounds and re-center.
- Enter Gas exchange. Verify practical tracing gates the question and correct answers gate the next investigation. Complete all four, then revisit and restart.
- Check keyboard, touch and small viewport layout. If Three.js or WebGL is unavailable, the labelled SVG fallback and all lesson controls remain usable.
